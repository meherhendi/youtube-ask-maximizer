YouTube's AI panel is too narrow.

This extension adds a button that makes it fill the whole window — and makes the text bigger. Click it again to put it back.

Before / after

Install

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in your browser.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Reload your YouTube tab.

Download — free

Use it

Open a video, click Ask under the player, then click the ⤢ button next to the panel's close button.

Click it again or press Esc to go back to normal.

Good to know

  • Free, and the code is public.
  • Asks for no permissions and collects nothing about you.
  • Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers.
  • Not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it installs manually as above.
